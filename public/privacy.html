M-Pay+ Pilot Registration — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Controller/contact: Gigapp Technologies Ltd., contact@mpayplus.co.ke

When you express interest in the M-Pay+ pilot, we collect your name, business name, telephone number, town or market, business type, existing payment method, and consent to contact you.

We use this information to assess pilot interest, determine eligibility, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, and contact you about the pilot. We do not open a payment account or process any payment through this form.

Form submissions are stored in a restricted Google Firebase/Cloud Firestore database. Anti-bot verification is processed using Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process security-related data in accordance with Cloudflare's policies. Authorized staff handling pilot recruitment may access your registration data.

We keep registrations for as long as reasonably necessary for pilot assessment and follow-up, subject to applicable legal requirements. Please email us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or to withdraw permission for further pilot contact. Legal rights may depend on applicable law.

We do not sell pilot registration details. This notice may be updated as the pilot evolves.
