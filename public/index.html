GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Everything you need to know.

Do I need to buy a POS terminal?

No. Your Android phone is the terminal. We'll confirm supported phones before the pilot.

Will customers need to install the M-Pay+ app?

Yes, the free M-Pay+ app, to tap phones and pay with M-Pesa or a card.

Where does the merchant's money go?

Straight to your account, through our licensed payment partners. We'll share settlement times before the pilot starts.

How much does M-Pay+ cost?

Customers pay nothing extra. Merchants pay a small fee per payment, at or below Lipa na M-Pesa for M-Pesa payments. No setup cost, no subscription.

What if a phone doesn't support NFC?

We're adding a QR code option for phones without NFC.

Is M-Pay+ live and licensed today?

Not yet. We're preparing a pilot in Nairobi. Payments will run through licensed payment partners, and M-Pay+ does not hold customer money.
